# Milan Pro brand (Windows XP era, 2002-2006)

Everything in this folder is generated by `tools/brand/` (see its README): Blender (headless) renders the icon
object, Python + Pillow + numpy does the XP finishing, the drawn small sizes, the type and the panels. No
third-party image or font ships; the type is set in Windows system fonts and rendered into the images. Edit the
scripts, not the PNGs.

## Research notes (2002-2006 Windows video software and icon style)

Notes in our own words from reading period documentation, reviews and press releases; no image was downloaded and
no mark, logo or text is reused.

- **Windows XP icon guidelines** (Microsoft, "Creating Windows XP Icons", July 2001). Icons are illustrated
  objects, not photos: rich colour that suits Luna; objects drawn at an angle in a fixed perspective for "energy";
  soft, slightly rounded edges and corners; light from the upper left with ambient fill; gradients for volume; an
  outline for definition on any background; a drop shadow (Photoshop: angle 135, distance 2, size 2, black at
  75 %). Sizes 48, 32 and 16 (24 for the Start menu); artwork at about 46/30/14 px so the shadow fits; the 16 px
  version is pixel-pushed and, if it does not read, simplified or shown straight-on. Single objects and symbols
  may be straight-on. No more than three objects; avoid letters and words in icons. The 4-bit fallback uses a
  black outline on the bottom/right edges and a dark grey one top/left.
- **Luna** (XP's visual style, 2001-2007): saturated royal blue taskbar and title bars, a green Start button,
  silver and olive alternatives, glossy rounded controls, Trebuchet MS bold title text, Tahoma 8 pt everywhere
  else. The classic desktop colour is #3A6EA5 and the 3D face grey #D4D0C8.
- **Video editors of the period** (Sonic Foundry / Sony Vegas 4-6 and Screenblast / Vegas Movie Studio,
  Premiere Pro 1.0/1.5 and Premiere Elements 1-2, Pinnacle Studio 8-10, Ulead VideoStudio 7-9 and MediaStudio
  Pro 7, Windows Movie Maker 2/2.1, Avid Xpress DV / Liquid 6, Nero 6/7, Roxio Easy Media Creator 7-8). Reviews
  from 2004-2005 describe refreshed, glossier interfaces (Pinnacle Studio 9's "new look", VideoStudio 9's
  full-screen layout, Movie Maker 2 built on the standard XP controls and styles). The recurring idioms of the
  consumer and prosumer boxes, splashes and icons of these years: an everyday film object (reel, filmstrip,
  clapperboard, camcorder, lens) rendered as a glossy 3D illustration; Luna blue with silver/brushed aluminium;
  one warm accent (orange or amber) against the blue; soft white swooshes and arcs and a soft glow behind the
  hero object; chrome-bevelled, often italic or extended sans wordmarks with a dark stroke and drop shadow;
  a small letter-spaced capitals descriptor; splash screens showing product name, version, a progress or status
  line and a copyright line, often on a lighter footer band.
- **Not the idiom**: the 2007 Vista "Aero" look (256 px photo-real hero renders, glass, wet-floor reflections)
  arrived at the end of the window and is used here only as a subtle highlight. Nothing of the 2020s (neon,
  circuit traces, grids, flat design, glassmorphism, mesh gradients).

## Directions explored

`python tools/brand/compose.py --directions out.png` renders three, each with icon (256/48/32/16), wordmark and
splash:

- **A. Film reel + play wedge** (chosen): a blue anodised film reel at the XP 3/4 angle, an orange play wedge in
  front, a short filmstrip tail. Trebuchet MS Bold Italic wordmark.
- B. Clapperboard with a chrome hinge: the most generic of the three; mostly black and white, so it loses its
  colour identity on the grey taskbar and at 16 px reduces to grey stripes. Franklin Gothic Heavy Italic.
- C. Lens orb with a filmstrip swoosh: the glossiest, which pulls it toward 2007 Vista glass rather than XP;
  at 16 px it becomes a dark blob. Verdana Bold Italic.

A wins because it follows the XP rules most directly (an everyday object at the XP angle, top-left light,
saturated blue with one complementary orange accent, at most three objects) and because it survives 16 px: a
blue disc with five dark windows and an orange triangle stays readable on white, on #3A6EA5 and on the grey
taskbar.

## The look

### Mark

A film reel with two Luna-blue anodised flanges (five round windows each, clear-coated), wound brown film between
them, a satin-silver hub, an orange glossy play wedge in front at the lower right, and (64 px and up) a filmstrip
tail with sky-blue frames curling out to the lower left. Light from the upper left (big soft key), a cool fill
from the right, a sky-over-horizon environment for the metal, Standard view transform (saturated, no filmic
roll-off).

Every size gets the XP finish: fitted into the size minus the shadow, a soft dark outline (the edge colour
darkened, 1 px at 48 px), and the 135 degree / 2 px / 75 % drop shadow scaled to the size.

- 16, 20, 24 px: drawn, not reduced (`TINY` in compose.py): straight-on reel on its own pixel grid, a back
  flange one pixel to the right for depth, 1 px dark outline, five windows, silver hub, the orange wedge with a
  1 px brown outline, a white glint at the top left, a 1 px soft shadow.
- 32, 40, 48, 64 px: from a separate "small" render (no tail, thicker flanges, larger wedge), sharpened, saturation
  raised, edge alpha hardened.
- 96, 128, 256, 512 px: from the 1024 px master render. The 256 is an XP icon scaled up, not a Vista hero render.

`milan-pro.svg` is a hand-written vector approximation (ellipses, gradients, a shadow filter).

### Wordmark

"Milan" in chrome and "Pro" in amber, Trebuchet MS Bold Italic (XP's own title-bar face), each with a horizon
gradient overlay, an inner bevel lit from the upper left, a dark navy stroke and a soft drop shadow: the
Photoshop layer styles of the period. Descriptor "NONLINEAR EDITING SYSTEM" in Tahoma Bold capitals,
letter-spaced, set at the final pixel size and right-aligned under the word. `wordmark-800.png` is transparent
(navy descriptor, for light backgrounds).

### Panels

Splash, About banner, README banner and installer side panels: a Luna-blue sky gradient, soft white swooshes
sweeping up to the right, a white glow behind the reel, faint brushed grain, and (splash, README, installer) a
brushed-aluminium footer with a bevel line, framed by a 1 px navy border.

The splash (520 x 320) keeps two places free for `src/ui/splash.rs`: the version text at the right of the
footer's first line (y 276) and the progress groove at y 300-306, x 24-496 (white groove, #7F9DB9 border, Luna
blue #316AC5 fill). The Start dialog shows the same art without them.

## Fonts

Windows system fonts only, rendered into the images: Trebuchet MS Bold Italic (`trebucbi.ttf`, wordmark), Tahoma
and Tahoma Bold (descriptor, legal line). The explorations also used Franklin Gothic Heavy Italic and Verdana
Bold Italic.
