## Method

- **Media.** Four 12 s clips made with ffmpeg lavfi: `testsrc2` 1920x1080 at 29.97 fps, each hue-shifted, H.264 (libx264 `veryfast`, GOP 250, yuv420p). One track per clip at Level 50 %, so every layer is decoded and blended; no proxies.
- **Playback** (`bench_playback`, `bench_playback_with_scopes`, `bench_playback_multicam_grid` in `src/engine/worker.rs`). The video worker plays the project at the default preview, Preview (Auto) at 960x540, against a 29.97 fps clock that starts with the first frame, as the audio clock drives it in the app. Like the Video Preview, the test takes a frame once its number is due, polling every 1 ms, for 240 frames.
  - *fps*: frames shown per second of wall time.
  - *Dropped*: frames never shown (the worker skipped them to catch up, or a newer one was already due), as a share of the frames due.
  - *Late layer frames*: layer pictures that missed their 25 ms deadline (the layer held its last picture) or that a decoder read past.
  - *Play start*: from Play to the first frame, decoders cold (each run starts a new worker).
  - *Video behind audio*: for each frame shown, the clock time when it went on screen less its own time: the preview pipeline's A/V offset, before the display and the audio device latency (which the player compensates, R9).
  - *Unpaced*: frames composed per second when nothing paces the worker (each frame waits for its decoders).
- **Scopes**: the same playback while a thread analyses a 960x540 frame with all four Video Scopes 10 times a second (Update Scopes while Playing).
- **Multicam grid**: the four clips made one multicamera track; the Video Preview shows the 2x2 take grid.
- **Scrub** (`bench_scrub`): 40 paused frame requests on one layer at 960x540, as dragging the cursor makes them: 30 forward steps, then jumps (300, 100, 250, 20) and steps after them. A jump in GOP-250 media respawns its decoder. *Steps back*: 60 single frames backwards from frame 300 (dragging left, J at -1x), read from chunks decoded forward (R20). Each also on the clip's video proxy (`proxy::args`: 1280 wide, a keyframe every 15 frames), which Preview reads.
- **Seeks** (`seeks_are_frame_exact_on_long_gop`): a 20 s 1920x1080 30 fps H.264 clip, one keyframe every 250 frames, with its frame number burnt in as 10 black or white blocks; 200 random paused frames at 960x540 (Good on the source, Preview on its proxy), each checked to be exactly the frame asked for.
- **Scrub drag** (`scrub_drag_follows_the_cursor`): the same clip, the cursor dragged at 2x for 3 s backwards and forwards through `VideoEngine` as the Video Preview drives it (60 Hz); at every UI frame, how far the frame on screen (read from its burnt-in number) is from the cursor.
- **Reverse playback** (`reverse_playback_shows_every_frame`): the same clip as a reversed event, played at 1x for 10 s against a 30 fps clock as above; a frame counts when the one on screen is exactly the source frame due.
- **Adjust Size and Quality for Optimal Playback** (`bench_adaptive_quality` in `src/engine/mod.rs`): the four layers at Best (Full), 1920x1080, looped for 60 s through the Video Preview itself (`decode::Preview`, a 60 Hz UI loop against the wall clock), with the option off and then on.

## Adjust Size and Quality for Optimal Playback (4 layers, Best (Full))

| Option | Dropped over 60 s | Dropped over the last 10 s | Setting changes | Setting at the end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| off | 66.31 % | 66.89 % | 0 | Best(Full) |
| on | 18.13 % | 0.00 % | 6 | Best(Quarter) |

With the option on, the preview steps down one rung (Full, Half, Quarter, then Draft) when more than 2 % of the frames in 2 s drop, and back up after 10 s without a drop; a step up that drops again doubles the wait before the next try (up to 160 s). Every change restarts the decoders at the new size, and on GOP-250 media that costs a stall of about 1-3 s, which the totals above include. The render never uses these settings.
